MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of
June 12, 2025 between Northwind Logistics Inc. (the "Customer") and Vantage
Grid Systems GmbH (the "Supplier").

1. SERVICES

1.1 The Supplier shall provide the route-optimisation platform described in
Schedule A (the "Services").

1.2 The Supplier may modify the scope, composition and technical
specification of the Services at any time at its sole discretion, and such
modification shall take effect on publication to the Supplier's website.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement commences on July 1, 2025 and continues for an initial
term of thirty-six (36) months.

2.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms
unless the Customer gives written notice of non-renewal not less than one
hundred and eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES

3.1 The Customer shall pay a platform fee of EUR 82,000 per month, exclusive
of value added tax.

3.2 The Supplier may adjust the fees at any time on thirty (30) days' notice,
and the Customer's continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance of
the adjusted fees.

3.3 The Customer shall pay all invoices within fifteen (15) days. Overdue
amounts accrue interest at 2.5% per month, compounded monthly.

4. DATA AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 All data generated through the Customer's use of the Services, including
route histories and derived models, shall be the sole and exclusive property
of the Supplier.

4.2 The Customer grants the Supplier a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide and
royalty-free licence to use, reproduce and commercialise any data the Customer
uploads to the Services, including after termination.

4.3 The Supplier shall have no obligation to return or delete Customer data on
termination.

5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 The Customer's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited
in respect of any breach of Section 4 or Section 8.

5.2 The Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Supplier
against any and all claims, losses, damages and expenses arising out of the
Customer's use of the Services, without limitation as to amount and without
regard to whether the claim arises from the Supplier's own negligence.

5.3 The Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed EUR 10,000 in
aggregate over the lifetime of this Agreement.

6. TERMINATION

6.1 The Customer may not terminate this Agreement for convenience.

6.2 On any termination the Customer shall pay, as an exit fee, the fees that
would have fallen due for the remainder of the then-current term.

7. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE

7.1 The Customer shall procure that each of its subcontractors, at every tier,
complies with the Supplier's security policy as amended from time to time, and
shall certify such compliance quarterly.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 The Customer shall keep the terms of this Agreement, including the fees,
confidential in perpetuity.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware and the
parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Delaware.
